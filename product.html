01 / EMG Signal

The electrical language of muscle.

Every time a muscle fiber contracts it fires an electrical spike. Actus reads that signal at 1,000 times per second, fast enough to catch the micro-fluctuations that predict fatigue, form breakdown, and injury risk before you feel them.

02 / Motion Intelligence

EMG is only half the picture.

Each sensor packs a 6-axis IMU: 3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope. Actus fuses muscle signal with segment orientation in real time, so the app knows not just which muscles are firing, but exactly how your body is moving when they do.
