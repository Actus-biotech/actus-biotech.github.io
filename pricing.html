Questions, answered.

Do I need a subscription?
The hardware and app work forever with the free tier. Pro unlocks per-muscle recovery scoring and coach-ready session reports for $12/mo — cancel any time, keep your hardware.
How does the sensor connect to my phone?
Each sensor pairs directly to your phone over Bluetooth. No hub or extra hardware is needed — just stick them on and open the Actus app. Multiple sensors connect simultaneously.
How accurate is the EMG data?
Sensors sample at 1 kHz with clinical-grade signal conditioning — the same EMG basis used in research labs. The IMU tracks segment orientation at the same rate for full motion context.
Can my coach or physio see my data?
Yes. Pro generates plain-language session reports you can share with a link or export as PDF. You can also grant your coach live-view access during a session.
What is your return policy?
30-day no-questions-asked returns on all hardware kits. Free shipping both ways. If you're not training better on signal in a month, send it back.
