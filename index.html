How it works

From muscle to insight
in three steps.

Sensor on skin
01

Place the sensors

Featherweight EMG sensors attach to target muscles in seconds. Place multiple sensors on any part of the body — quads, hamstrings, calves, shoulders, arms — for full-body coverage.

Actus sensor + IMU
02

Connect to your phone

Each sensor streams EMG and accelerometer/IMU data to your phone at 1 kHz with sub-20 ms latency — no hub, no wires, no complicated setup.

App in hand
03

Read your signal

The Actus app translates raw data into calm, glanceable guidance — what to push, what to ease, and what to recover — rep by rep, muscle by muscle.
