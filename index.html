Why Actus

Science meets intelligence,
on every rep.

Most training is still based on guesswork. Actus gives you clinical-grade muscle data and the AI to act on it.

01

Realtime Informed Feedback

Know exactly which muscles are firing, how hard, and whether your form is degrading, all while you move. Actus delivers per-muscle feedback on activation and fatigue in under 20 ms, so every rep is informed, not assumed.

02

Recovery Tracking & Injury Prevention

Measure how your muscles recover between sessions. Actus detects compensation patterns and muscle imbalances before they escalate into injury, essential for athletes returning from rehab and those pushing performance limits.

03

Optimizing Personalized Workout Programs

Every session generates objective data that the Actus AI uses to adapt your training. Progressive overload grounded in what your muscles actually tolerated, not generic progression tables. Train smarter, build faster.

How it works

From muscle to insight
in three steps.

Sensor on skin
01

Place the sensors

Featherweight EMG sensors attach to target muscles in seconds. Place up to 6 sensors anywhere on the body: quads, hamstrings, shoulders, arms, for complete muscle coverage.

Wireless stream
02

Connect to your phone

Sensors stream EMG and 6-axis IMU data wirelessly to the Actus app at 1 kHz with sub-20 ms latency. No hub, no wires, no complicated setup. Just open the app and train.

App guidance
03

Read your signal

The Actus app and AI translate raw data into calm, glanceable guidance: what muscles to push, what to protect, and how to recover, rep by rep, session by session.
